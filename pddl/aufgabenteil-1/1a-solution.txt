There are a lot of things that are unusual about this plan:
- Parcel 1 is not loaded alongside Parcel 3 through 5, which means a separate trip to warehouse 2 must be made just for Parcel 1. Additionally, it is loaded as part of an unrelated trip to warehouse 4.
- The same thing happens to Parcel 2. It is not loaded alongside Parcel 6 and 8, which means a separate trip to warehouse 4 must be made just for Parcel 2. Additionally, it is loaded as part of an unrelated trip to warehouse 2.
- Parcel 7 requires its own trip because it is the only parcel intended for warehouse 3. However, it is loaded early on and unloaded last, which led to it seeing all of the first four warehouses.
- Berger and Meyer switch twice when either of them could have done the entire unloading process themselves.
- Both trucks drive to warehouse 4 in quick succession, when truck 2 could have been loaded with truck 1's contents first, saving a trip.
- Meyer and Schmitz switch trucks at warehouse 4 when they could just have retained their respective trucks.
- After the above switch, truck 1 goes on to make two trips whereas Schmitz and truck 2 remain unused at warehouse 4. They could have split the two trips across the two trucks.
