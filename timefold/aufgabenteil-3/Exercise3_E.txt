With maximizeScheduledLessons, EDV_01 and Malerei are fully assigned as these are the courses with the highest demand.
EDV_01 is put into Room 2 as that one has the highest capacity, and Malerei receives Room 1 with the second-highest capacity.
The other courses are filled in where needed in order to maximize the teachers' capacities.

maximizeRevenue turns things around, however. Tonformen and Malerei are now fully assigned and put into highest-capacity Room 2, because their entry fees are highest.
The course with the other second-highest entry-fee, EDV_02, is not assigned more because the relatively low demand holds it back. Assigning courses no one takes is of no value.
Webdesign and EDV_01 then fill up leftover slots, with Webdesign taking the remaining slots in Room 2 because it has the greater entry fee.
